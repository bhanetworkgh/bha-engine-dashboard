# The contrast checklist

Added 13 Nov 2026 (Destiny) from Jason's vFarm messaging doctrine of 27 Oct 2026 (SOP f0a871ac, "SOP -- vFarm Messaging Doctrine: Contrast Block & Checklist for Media Twin"; watch DW-1793095093376-AZUR). Rebuilt 14 Nov 2026 after n8n's update dropped the skill; the rule is unchanged.

## Before any vFarm draft or rewrite
Ask Jason's two questions of every platform's text:
1. Does this piece clearly say why typical vertical farms and home units collect dust (hard to set up, hard to run, high maintenance, science projects people stop using) and how vFarm avoids that?
2. Does it position vFarm as grab-and-go, like an appliance, not a hobby?

## What you do with the answers
- Write each platform's answer to both questions in `self_check`, one short line per platform.
- A "no" is rewritten before you answer, unless the post is an occasion post where the contrast would not fit; then say so in `self_check`.
- Keep it true to the product: never over-promise full autonomy. The direction is less babysitting, with more of the watching and looking after done for the owner, and anything not proven yet is said as what we are building (see "Target versus status").
- The contrast is Jason's default pattern, not a fixed sentence: say it in the post's own words, never paste the same line every day.
