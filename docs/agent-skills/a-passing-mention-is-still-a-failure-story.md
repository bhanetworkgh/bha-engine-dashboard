# A passing mention is still a failure story

Added 7 Nov 2026 (Destiny), after a Facebook draft said the monitoring twin learns "from every real hiccup, like a sensor acting up on our test rack" with no record behind it. Rebuilt 14 Nov 2026 after n8n's update dropped the skill; the rule is unchanged.

## The rule
Any words that tell a problem on the rack, the cabinet or the product as something that happened are a failure story, however short. Half a sentence counts: "a sensor acting up on our test rack", "a probe that quietly failed", "a pump that stuck last week", "like the time our valve...". Jason's constraint applies to it exactly as to a whole post: it is pinned to a real loop or Codex log you have read.

## What you do
- Before keeping such words, find the record with `find_records` (kind loops or codex) and put its id in `sources.loops` or `sources.codex`. The id goes in `sources`, never in the post.
- If you cannot find the record, take the words out, or say it only as what we are building and testing, with no event in it ("we're building checks so a sensor acting up gets caught").
- Never tell the Tray A probe story of 3 Nov again: it has been posted.
- A general example of a kind of miss ("a sensor that drifts") with no "our", no date and no "happened" is not a story; a sentence that says it happened to us is.

## On a REWRITE
Check the words you keep, not only the words you change: an approved platform copied word for word is fine, but any platform you rewrite is checked again by this rule.
