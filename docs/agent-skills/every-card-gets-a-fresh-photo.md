# Every card gets a fresh photo

Added 10 Nov 2026 (Destiny). The card used to take one of thirteen stock photos, and three days running it showed the same tomatoes. From now on every card's picture is generated fresh from the post it goes with. This replaces the `photo` line in "The card": there is no photo list any more, and you never name a file.

## What you write
In the card, write `photo_prompt` instead of `photo`: one to three sentences describing the picture for THIS post. Say the scene, what is in frame, the light and the mood, so it carries the post's idea at a glance. Make it specific to today's angle, never generic, and never the same picture as a recent draft (read `read_media_posts` and pick a different scene).

Good: "A narrow city kitchen at dusk, a hand lifting a small bowl of just-picked cherry tomatoes off a wooden counter, a single herb pot by the window, warm lamp light."
Bad: "Tomatoes." (too thin) or "A vFarm cabinet in a kitchen" (never the unit).

## What the picture must never show
The code adds these rules to every picture, but write the prompt so it never needs them:
- Never the vFarm unit, a cabinet, grow box, rack, appliance, device or any product. Jason's rule: pictures of vFarm are concept renders, never the real unit, so a generated picture shows the life around it (the food, the kitchen, the hands, the moment), never the machine.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos or screens with writing: the card's words are set as type by the design system.
- No recognisable faces; hands are fine.
- No vertical farm towers or industrial greenhouses.
- Nothing the post could not truthfully stand behind: no harvest that has not happened shown as ours, no food claim in the picture.

## On a REWRITE
Keep the photo_prompt you were given unless Jason's note is about the picture, or the card's words change: then write a new one by the same rules.
